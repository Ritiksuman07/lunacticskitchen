# Development Roadmap (10 Weeks)

## Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4): Daily Order MVP

### Deliverables
- Mobile-first menu with “Under ₹100” and combos.
- Cart + checkout with transparent tax/delivery breakup.
- Phone OTP authentication.
- Razorpay UPI integration.
- Free-water auto-add promotion logic.

### Success Criteria
- User can complete order in under 90 seconds from menu to payment.
- Payment success and webhook reconciliation above 99%.
- No discrepancy between displayed and charged amount.

## Phase 2 (Weeks 5-6): Operations Layer

### Deliverables
- Kitchen Display System with real-time queue lanes.
- SOP panel per ordered item.
- Ingredient stock tracking and sold-out synchronization.

### Success Criteria
- KDS receives new paid orders in near-real-time (<3s).
- Low stock alerts generated before stockout for key budget items.

## Phase 3 (Weeks 7-10): Growth Engines

### Deliverables
- Subscription plan builder (7/15/30 days, lunch/dinner).
- Skip with 4-hour cutoff and wallet credit.
- Bulk order portal with 7-day lockout and 200-unit cap.

### Success Criteria
- Subscription management self-serve completion above 80%.
- Bulk booking validation blocks all out-of-policy requests.

## Phase 4 (Post-MVP): Optimization & Scale

### Deliverables
- Repeat-order analytics dashboard and cohort views.
- Segment-based promotions (student/corporate/hostel).
- SOP and prep-time optimization based on kitchen telemetry.

### Success Criteria
- Repeat order rate month-over-month growth.
- Prep-time reduction for high-volume SKUs.
